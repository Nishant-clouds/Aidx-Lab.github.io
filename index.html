01 / About

About the Lab

The Language & Society Research Lab investigates the social impact of natural language processing. Our work sits at the intersection of machine learning, linguistics and public policy.

We believe language technology should be accountable to the communities it affects. That principle shapes how we choose problems, build datasets, and evaluate systems — prioritising transparency, reproducibility and real-world deployment over benchmark scores alone.

  • Open datasets and reproducible research
  • Community-centred evaluation practices
  • Cross-disciplinary collaboration
